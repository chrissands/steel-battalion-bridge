Calibration

  1. Select Start.
  2. Move both levers, the sight-change stick and all three pedals through their full range a few times.
  3. Let go of everything (levers centred, pedals released) and select Finish. The centre is taken from the resting position.

About

Steel Battalion Bridge turns the controller into a virtual Xbox 360 controller, which Steam Input and games see like a real one. An Xbox pad has 15 buttons and 6 axes. The cockpit's flight controls go to the pad, and the rest go to keyboard keys you bind in-game, or to a Shift layer.
